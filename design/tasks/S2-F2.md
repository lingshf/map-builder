# S2-F2: second real-data demo preset for elevated parts, passages and roofs

Owner: dev · sprint S2 · **v0.3.0 blocker** · recorded in [S2-REVIEW](../reviews/S2-REVIEW.md)
· **D49 (coordinator): option (a)**, a second real-data preset (S2-REVIEW §5)

## Problem

The v2 goal and the REL-v0.3.0 acceptance criteria need a drone capsule (r = 1 m) to pass
beneath a **real** elevated part or building passage. The D26 Pittsburgh area has none. Roof
shapes have not been checked on real data at all.

## Scope

1. **Find the area (time box: half a day).** Use Overpass (OSM, ODbL) to find an area of at
   most 1 × 1 km (D3) with:
   * at least one `building:part` or `building` with `min_height` or `building:min_level`;
   * at least one highway with `tunnel=building_passage`;
   * at least 5 buildings with a supported non-flat `roof:shape`.

   Prefer an area with GLO-30 coverage and a modest building count. List the candidates and
   their feature counts in the PR body.
2. **Add the preset** `tools/demo/presets/<area>.json`, following the `pittsburgh.json` schema.
   Include cameras for an `elevated-under` view, a `passage` view and a `roofs` close-up.
3. **Write the acceptance cases first** (DoD 10). Add real-data excerpts (OSM ids from the area)
   as unit tests. Include at least one counterexample that must **not** yield a probe, such as
   a courtyard or a dead-end passage.
4. **Build the package** through the CLI with live GLO-30 + Overpass. Run it through the Python
   validator and the C# reader (DoD 9), and attach both results.

If no candidate is found within the time box, stop and report: per D49 this falls back to
option (c), synthetic-only evidence, and the REL-v0.3.0 release notes must state that limit.
Do not widen the scope. Any generator defect found becomes S2-F5.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The new preset builds `VALID` under strict validation, with default content (D30, D32).
2. At least one elevated-part or building-passage probe is exported, and it PASSES in both
   Python and C#, with no query-budget warning and no `mb_id` warning.
3. The roof shapes present in the area are generated as their tagged shape, not as a flat
   fallback, or the fallback reason is reported. Give counts per shape in the PR.
4. The real-data excerpt tests run offline from committed excerpts, each ≤ 5 s.
5. `tests/test_demo_bundle.py` accepts the new preset.

## Performance considerations

Build time (excluding download) ≤ 120 s, the median of 3 runs (D32). Record triangles, objects
and probe-check visits. Pick a smaller area rather than optimising.

## Conflict domain

Hard: `tools/demo/presets/<area>.json` (new), `tests/test_demo_bundle.py` (preset rows only),
new excerpt tests under `tests/` with fixtures under `tests/fixtures/<area>_*`. No product code.
