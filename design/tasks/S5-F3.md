## Shore-step bound (decided here)

The bound is **1.5 m + 1 mm**. The 1.5 m is the designed carve step (D115, S4-COL-FLOAT-2). The 1 mm covers float
error only. Float32 positions at map coordinates under 2 km carry about 1e-4 m of error, and the reference maximum
on Pittsburgh was exactly 1.500 m. Factoria's **1.548 m exceeds the bound by 48 mm**, which is far beyond float
error, so it is a **class D candidate**.

Step 1 must explain it. Raising the bound is **not** in scope for this task. If the explanation is a designed
geometry the spec did not foresee (for example the audit ring crossing a ramp slope), that is a new design decision
recorded by Product/Design before S5-F3 closes. Until then the region counts as D.

## Step 1: investigation (read-only; may start now, in parallel)

No product or tool edits are made in this step. The result is posted in the PR, or as an issue comment, before
any fix.

**Measurand.** This step uses the audit's own definitions (`tools/floating_audit.py`, the cell centre at the audit
`cell` spacing and a downward ray, threshold 0.2 m). For every region, at every cell of the region, it compares the
following four Z values:

* the **collision** hit: top hit, any class, through `core/query.py`;
* the **visual** hit, through the GLB;
* the **water sheet** level (`water:<id>`) at that XY;
* the **outside terrain**: the terrain or `bank:` surface just outside the sheet, as the audit defines it for that
  category.

**Per-region evidence, required for every region and every class.** A label alone is not accepted.

* The region id, category and bbox; the centre XY and every cell XY.
* The objects hit, with their `mb_id` and class: the collision top hit, the visual top hit, the water body and the
  terrain or `bank:` object.
* The four Z values above at each cell, and the resulting offset.
* **Python and C#** reader rays at the centre and on a ±0.5 m grid at 0.1 m spacing, giving the hit object and Z.
  Both readers must agree.
* For `water_above_bank`: whether a `bank:` ramp exists there; if it is missing, the `shore_ramp_blocked_m` entry and
  the mask that blocked it, or the reason no ramp was attempted (for example a body type or junction #77 did not
  cover, a standalone `water=*` pond, or the terrain edge).
* For `water_shore_step`: whether the region lies on the designed 1 m audit ring, and its maximum against the bound.
* For heightfield findings: the category, and why it is or is not the designed D117 behaviour (`.mbhf` shows the
  bed) or edge coverage.

**Classes:**

* **D, real defect.** Judged **per surface**: the water sheet against the bank or outside terrain, checked
  **separately on the collision surface** (`.mbcol` water vs `.mbcol` terrain or `bank:`) and **on the visual
  surface** (GLB water vs GLB terrain or `bank:`). It is not collision against visual. A region is D if, on either
  surface:
  * the sheet hangs above the outside surface by more than the threshold where #77 requires a ramp or cascade;
  * the sheet is buried below it;
  * a shore step exceeds the bound.
* **E, designed behaviour:** matches an accepted class (the shore step within the bound, `hf_below_ground:water`)
  with the evidence above.
* **F, audit artefact or edge ambiguity:** on both surfaces the sheet and bank relation is correct per the
  evidence, and the audit misclassifies it (the D159 kind).

**Stop and escalate** to the Team Leader if:

* the Python and C# readers disagree;
* the evidence proves none of D, E or F;
* a defect needs a geometry change outside the water stage.

## Step 2: changes, by class

* **D:** fix at the source in the water or shore stage, using the exact predicates of S4-COL-FLOAT-2 (AC2 and AC3).
  No masking, no audit-only exemption for a real defect, and no loosened threshold. Serialised after S5-PERF-GATE.
* **F:** an audit-only change to the audit tool and its tests, under S4-F8 case-A rules: every package stays
  byte-identical. The change must make the **measured** audit count zero for those regions. It needs the
  per-region evidence, plus a **negative test**: a genuine sheet-above-bank defect placed next to the reclassified
  pattern must still be flagged.
* **E:** no change; the evidence is the deliverable.

## Protected data (needed before any class-D change merges)

Collision, heightfield and the classic output are protected (G0, D141). A class-D change needs:

1. **Explicit user approval, given before merge** (G0, D145). The Team Leader asks the user with `ask_user`,
   presenting the per-region evidence and every affected object (`mb_id`, class, the surfaces and members that
   change). A D-number records that user grant. Coordinator-only authorisation is **not** sufficient.
2. **The #77 invariants kept:**
   * bed depth −1.5 m;
   * wet clearance (D116);
   * decks never lowered;
   * the `tools/water_replay.py` §7 replay with Python == C# on every query, every difference listed.
3. **A rebaseline rule** (applies only if packages changed):
   * S5-STYLE-BASE's AC1 classic baseline is re-recorded on the post-fix main;
   * every later S5 task uses the new main as its G0 base;
   * classic-identity (D141) comparisons restart from that main;
   * the old baseline is kept in the record.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Classification complete.** Every region from the #85 report is classified D, E or F, with the per-region
   evidence above.
2. **Measured zero gate, whatever the D/E/F mix.** S5-F3 closes only when `tools/floating_audit.py`, run on the
   merged main, **measures** `water_above_bank` = 0 and `water_buried` = 0 on Factoria, Pittsburgh, Munich and the
   sample, through both the CLI and Blender. A classification never replaces the measured zero.
3. **Shore step:** max ≤ 1.5 m + 1 mm everywhere, or a recorded design decision that changes the bound (see above).
4. **Heightfield:** every remaining finding is class E with a stated reason.
5. **Package invariants:**
   * **D:** member-level comparison as in S4-F8 case B. `.mbcol` changes only in the permitted water, `bank:` and
     cascade objects. `.mbhf` and the georeference are unchanged unless the permission record says otherwise.
     Probes and the blocked and dropped lists are unchanged. Packages without the defect are whole-archive
     byte-identical.
   * **F, or E only:** every package is whole-archive byte-identical.
6. **Closure without a generator fix.** If every region is E or F:
   * the task closes with the audit-only change (F), after the step-1 evidence is posted and reviewed, or with no
     change if no region is in a zero-gated category;
   * AC2 still has to be **measured** at zero after the audit change, together with its negative test (step 2, F).
7. **Performance:** the D153 gate (S5-PERF-GATE merges before any step-2 change), with Munich and Pittsburgh as the
   gating presets.
8. **Tests (≤ 5 s each):**
   * D: one synthetic regression per defect pattern, failing before the fix;
   * F: one audit test per misclassification pattern, failing before the fix;
   * no duplicates of S4-COL-FLOAT-2 or S4-F8 cases.
