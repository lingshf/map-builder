## Finding (Team Leader report, #85)

`tools/floating_audit.py` (threshold 0.2 m), run on the **classic** Bellevue-Factoria build (D157 preset, frozen
caches, offline, seed 1, DSM on), reports:

| Category | Factoria classic | Contract |
|---|---|---|
| `water_above_bank` | **4** | must be **0** (S4-COL-FLOAT-2 AC1, #77) |
| `water_shore_step` | 119 regions | information; the designed shore step, max ≤ 1.5 m (as on Pittsburgh: 104 regions, max 1.500 m) |
| heightfield findings | 5 regions | not yet classified |

Exact regions, objects and maximum offsets for each category are in the #85 PR body. This is an **audit finding
only**. Its impact on the real query and on a drone is unproven until step 1 (D149, D159 lesson).

## Step 1: investigation (read-only, may run now in parallel)

No product edits are made in this step. Post the result in the PR (or an issue comment) before any fix.

1. For each `water_above_bank` region, report:
   * the body id;
   * the sheet level;
   * the outside-ground Z;
   * the step size;
   * whether a `bank:` ramp exists there;
   * why it was blocked, if it was (`shore_ramp_blocked_m` and the mask it hit), or whether this is a body or
     junction case #77 did not cover (for example a standalone `water=*` pond, a stream polygon, or a body at the
     terrain edge).
2. Cast the **package's own query** (Python and C#) at each region centre and on a ±0.5 m grid. Report whether
   the collision surface is really above the visible ground outside the sheet (a real defect), or whether this is
   an audit classification or edge case (D159 kind).
3. For each `water_shore_step` region, confirm the maximum is ≤ 1.5 m and that the region lies on the designed
   1 m audit ring. Any step > 1.5 m is reported as a defect.
4. Classify each heightfield finding:
   * `hf_below_ground:water`: by design `.mbhf` shows the bed (D117), so it is accepted;
   * `hf_coverage`: edge cells;
   * anything else: report it.
5. **Classify every region** as one of:
   * **(D) real defect**, for example a collision surface hanging above the ground;
   * **(E) designed behaviour**, matching the accepted Pittsburgh class;
   * **(F) audit artefact or edge ambiguity**.

**Stop and escalate** to the Team Leader if:

* the Python and C# readers disagree;
* the result is inconclusive;
* a defect needs a geometry change outside the water stage.

## Step 2: fix (only for class D; serialised after S5-PERF-GATE)

* Fix at the source in the water or shore stage, using the exact predicates of S4-COL-FLOAT-2 (AC2 and AC3). No
  masking, no audit-only exemption for a real defect, and no loosened threshold.
* For class F, the audit fix follows S4-F8's case-A rules: only the tool and its tests change, and every package
  stays byte-identical.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Classification is complete.** Every region from the #85 report is classified D, E or F, with evidence.
2. **After the fix:**
   * `water_above_bank` = **0**, and `water_buried` = **0**, on Factoria, Pittsburgh, Munich and the sample, through
     the CLI and Blender;
   * the `water_shore_step` maximum is ≤ 1.5 m everywhere;
   * any remaining heightfield finding is class E with a stated reason.
3. **G0 for unaffected packages.** Packages without the defect (Munich, and the sample unless step 1 finds the
   pattern there) are whole-archive byte-identical. For changed packages, the comparison goes member by member, as
   in S4-F8 case B:
   * `.mbhf` and the georeference are unchanged unless the fix requires otherwise, with a stated reason;
   * `.mbcol` changes only in the affected water, `bank:` and cascade objects;
   * the fixed-query replay (`tools/water_replay.py`, Python and C#) has differences only there;
   * probes and the blocked and dropped lists are unchanged.
4. **Performance:** the D153 gate (or D147 before it merges), with Munich and Pittsburgh as the gating presets.
5. **Tests (≤ 5 s each):** one synthetic regression per defect pattern found, failing before the fix. No duplicates
   of the S4-COL-FLOAT-2 cases.
