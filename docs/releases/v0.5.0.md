# v0.5.0 - rivers, roof ends and release hygiene

Release-candidate content rebuilt on the rebased REL-v0.5.0 head (after #77 and #81).
Rivers inside the map are now **flat water surfaces** on a carved bed instead
of terrain painted blue that followed the dip of the elevation data. More
gabled roofs are built as tagged, and the release is easier to reproduce. The
package schema stays **2.1.0**.

> Floating-collision audit is clean for all rebuilt packages except the
> disclosed pre-existing S4-F8 Pittsburgh CLI singleton
> (`osm:way/537242388`, `buried_collision`).

## What is new

### Rivers and lakes are water surfaces (#70, #72, #74, #61)

- **Flat water on a carved bed** (S4-WATER-SURFACE, #72, D112-D118). Each
  water body inside the map gets its own mesh, `water:osm:<id>`, with the
  material **MB Water**, clipped to the exact OSM polygon. Its level comes
  from the shore; long rivers (300 m or more) follow a level profile that
  never rises downstream. The terrain under the water is carved 1.5 m below
  the surface and the banks are blended, so water no longer follows the
  elevation dip with 30 m staircase edges. In v0.4.0 the river was terrain
  coloured blue-green; nothing was a water surface.
- **Water is collision class 5 (`water`).** Rays and capsules over a river now
  hit the water surface in `.mbcol`. The heightfield `.mbhf` stays
  terrain-only and shows the carved **riverbed** (D117), so query `.mbcol`
  over water. Your simulator decides what touching water means.
- **Bridges and piers respect the water.** Decks are never lowered. A deck is
  raised only where the water would touch it, and listed in `report.json`
  `water_decks_raised` (Pittsburgh: way/11950069 +0.231 m, way/173225719
  +1.149 m). Piers and building walls extend down to the carved bed.
- **Every body is reported.** `report.json` `water_bodies` gives each body's
  outcome: `built(flat)`, `built(profile, N stations)` or a named fallback to
  painted terrain cells, and the roads that cross the water at grade.
- **The bank keeps its landcover colour** (S4-WATER-BANK, #74). Terrain cells
  next to a built water surface no longer take the water colour, so there is
  no blue saw-tooth on the bank above the water line. Colours only: collision
  and heightfield are unchanged.
- **Water reads blue** (S4-TEX-WATER, #61, D99). The terrain detail texture is
  now neutral grey, so it no longer tints rivers green. The water mesh itself
  is untextured (a water texture is S5-TEX-WATER, D124).

### Roofs (#60, #62, #67)

- **Vertical-cut gable ends** (S4-ROOF-ENDS, #67). A gable end that v0.4.0
  could not represent (the ridge met the end outside its edge, or the end was
  a sliver) is now closed by a vertical wall through the end edge instead of
  turning the whole roof hipped. A gabled roof keeps at least two gable ends;
  any remaining end stays hipped.
- **Near-rectangle gables** (S4-ROOF-NAMED, #62) are built exactly, and every
  roof that still falls back names its reason in `provenance.roof_shape`.
- A concave footprint tagged `roof:shape=pyramidal` is now a named flat
  fallback.

### Speed, reproducibility and housekeeping

- **Packaging and UV pipeline updates** (S4-PERF-PKG #63, S4-PERF-UV #64):
  single-pass compression and UV-path changes, with byte-identical output.
- **Fewer degenerate collision triangles** (S4-COL-DEGEN, #71, D98, D111).
  Road outlines drop duplicate and collinear points, and faces with no area
  are dropped. The few that remain can still be hit by a ray, so they are kept,
  and `report.json` lists each one per object (`degenerate_objects`).
- **Blender hygiene** (S4-BLENDER-HYGIENE, #66). The bounding-box edges are
  text fields read in double precision (the old fields could move an edge by
  up to 0.24 m); a bbox saved by v0.4.0 migrates automatically. An unmodified
  default material is exported as "MB Default".
- **Extension readiness** (S4-EXT-READY, #69, D100). The extension zip is
  reproducible: two clean builds of the same commit are byte-identical. The
  release zip built in CI is checked against a local clean build at
  publication.
  [docs/PLATFORM_SUBMISSION.md](../PLATFORM_SUBMISSION.md) lists what a
  Blender Extensions Platform submission needs.
- **Format policy** (S4-FORMAT-POLICY, #68, D101, D110). A **draft**
  compatibility and versioning policy for `.mbmap` is in the
  [package format](../PACKAGE_FORMAT.md) stability section. The schema itself
  is unchanged (2.1.0).
- **Licence** (D13): the Blender add-on (`map_builder/blender/`) is
  GPL-3.0-or-later and everything else we wrote is Apache-2.0; the extension
  zip ships the licence texts. Textures stay CC0.
- Developer: hermetic C# tests with a repository `NuGet.config`, and a guard
  against leaked file handles (S4-TEST-HYGIENE, #65).
- **Shore ramps** (S4-COL-FLOAT-2, #77): collision shoreline ramps are enabled
  and validated in the rebuilt release candidates.
- **Mypy CI gate** (S4-MYPY, #81): the tree is mypy-clean and CI now enforces
  that with a required mypy check.

Planning and review PRs: #59 (sprint plan), #60 (roof spike, closed scratch:
it measured 29 to 45), #70 (water spec), #73 (S4-REVIEW).

## Roofs: what to expect

On the frozen Munich Altstadt snapshot, **45 of 67** tagged non-flat roofs are
built as tagged (v0.4.0: 29; v0.3.0 under the same checker: 1). That meets
both the floor of 29 and the stretch goal of 45 (D105).

| Tagged shape | v0.5.0 | v0.4.0 |
|---|---|---|
| gabled | 33 / 52 | 17 / 52 |
| hipped | 11 / 12 | 11 / 12 |
| skillion | 1 / 1 | 1 / 1 |
| pyramidal | 0 / 1 | 0 / 1 |
| quadruple_saltbox | 0 / 1 | 0 / 1 |
| **total** | **45 / 67** | **29 / 67** |

All 22 others carry a named reason: 18 gabled roofs are built hipped because
no gable end is representable even with a vertical cut, and 4 are built flat
(a courtyard, `quadruple_saltbox`, roof height 0, a concave pyramid). The four
unnamed analytic gables of v0.4.0 (S3-F1) are now built exactly. Of the
built roofs, 28 are full skeleton roofs, 30 have a vertical gable wall on 2
edges and 3 on 3 edges. **Mixed ends** (one vertical-cut end and one hipped
end on the same roof) are covered by synthetic tests only; no real Munich roof
has one.

### Pittsburgh passage probes: 13 to 11

The water changes two Pittsburgh probes. Both are listed in `report.json`
`passage_probes_blocked` with the blocking object:

- `osm:way/11950069:under` moved about 8 m onto a path that a pier of the
  neighbouring bridge way/121593794 blocks.
- `osm:way/121593794:deck` now hits the river at its abutment
  (`water:osm:relation/7937544`; the capsule reaches 0.53 m below the water).

Every other v0.4.0 probe survives and passes in Python and C#. Probes over
water clear the surface by at least 0.62 m.

### Performance against v0.4.0 (D147, D150, D151)

Two methods are recorded and interpreted separately:

- **CPU time method (D147):** `time.process_time` on frozen Pittsburgh replay
  (`18f0822` vs REL head), 5 alternating rounds on this shared host. The paired
  medians are CLI whole Generate **0.95x** and Blender Generate+Export
  **0.99x**. Identical-code re-runs on this host show a spread of
  approximately **0.65x to 1.19x**, so this method is treated as noisy here and
  no timing claim is made.
- **Deterministic work method (D151):** cProfile primitive call counts show
  **about 13% more deterministic work in Blender Generate than v0.4.0, all in
  the new water stage**: water surfaces (#72) about **+7%** and shore ramps
  (#77) about **+5%**.

This is a **disclosed release exception (D151)**, tracked for follow-up as
**S5-PERF-WATER**.

## How to try it

1. Download `map_builder-0.5.0.zip` from this release's assets (not the
   automatic Source code archive). In Blender 4.2 LTS: **Edit > Preferences >
   Get Extensions > dropdown > Install from Disk...**, select the zip, and
   enable **Map Builder**.
2. Enable **Edit > Preferences > System > Network > Allow Online Access**.
3. Open **3D Viewport > Sidebar (N) > Map Builder**. Choose **Centre + size**,
   **40.437 / -80.016**, **1200 x 1000 m** (Pittsburgh, the Monongahela by the
   Fort Pitt Bridge).
4. Click **Generate Map** and look at the river: a flat `water:osm:…` surface
   under the bridges. Set an output path, click **Export Map Package**, then
   **Validate Package**.

No Blender? Download `pittsburgh-0.5.0.mbmap` and check it from the v0.5.0
source root:

```powershell
py -3.12 tools\mbmap_cli.py validate pittsburgh-0.5.0.mbmap
dotnet run -c Release --project examples\csharp\MbmapReader -- pittsburgh-0.5.0.mbmap
```

Expected: `VALID: 0 error(s), 1 warning(s)` (degenerate triangles), and from
the C# reader `OK` with all 11 passage probes PASS.

## Compatibility

- Extension **0.5.0**; package schema **2.1.0**, unchanged. `.mbcol` stays
  format 2 and the heightfield format 1. Water uses collision class 5, which
  the format already defined. v0.3.0 and v0.4.0 readers accept v0.5.0
  packages. See the draft [stability policy](../PACKAGE_FORMAT.md).
- **package_id changes.** Packages report `generator.version` **0.5.0**, so
  the same inputs give a new `package_id`. The committed synthetic sample was
  regenerated.
- A consumer that reads `.mbhf` over a river now sees the carved bed, not the
  water surface. Use `.mbcol` for anything that must touch the water.

## Known limitations and follow-ups

- **Known issue S4-F8:** the Pittsburgh CLI floating audit still reports one
  pre-existing buried singleton (`osm:way/537242388`), documented and unchanged.
- **Degenerate triangles:** listed and kept (see above); they do not affect
  validity. A v1.0 release needs a decision on this exception (D102).
- **Water coverage:** only water areas tagged in the supported way are built.
  Standalone `water=*`, riverbank multipolygons and coastline are not yet
  covered, and a body with no part inside the terrain is a named fallback
  (Pittsburgh way/1037461399, `fallback(no shore samples)`) (S4-F3).
- **Mixed roof ends** are tested synthetically only.
- **Performance known item / disclosed release exception (D151):** Blender
  Generate does about 13% more deterministic work (Python call counts) than
  v0.4.0, attributed to the new water stage; tracked for **S5-PERF-WATER**.
- Unchanged: highway structures only; probes are capsule sanity checks; edits
  are not re-probed, so a blocked probe is a warning and the package is still
  `VALID` (treat it as a stop); the simulator's map loader is consumer-side work.
